Sellout Watcher

How fast do Park & Rec classes sell out? Plan registration day instead of refreshing in a panic.

Hello, I'm MK, an Arlington parent. I've missed a few class registrations for my kid over the last few seasons because I assumed logging in twenty minutes after the window opened was fine. It turned out some sections sell out in seconds while others sit open for weeks, and the catalog gives no signal which is which! I built this site to give other residents that visibility.

Questions or suggestions: selloutwatcher@gmail.com

For example, in Arlington's Summer 2026 window

  • Gymnastics for ages 3–6 sold out in the first poll cycle — under 20 minutes, many likely under a minute.
  • Weekend morning swim slots (Sat 8:30–11:30am) were the most competitive time-and-day combo.
  • A few adult fitness classes at Langston Brown (Body Sculpting, Barre) also went in the first cycle.
  • Ceramics, martial arts, music, sports leagues, science, woodworking — near-zero sellouts. Safe to register next week.

How the data works

Each county's public registration system is checked on a schedule that ramps up around the time a window opens and tapers off in the days after. We record each section's status (Available / Waitlist / Full) over time and derive how long it took to sell out.

Anything that sold out faster than we could measure shows up as "Instant (<20 min)" — treat that as a "you needed to be ready before noon" floor, not a measured duration.
